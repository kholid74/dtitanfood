OWN-WORLD: Uncoated warm ivory as the ground; chocolate ink; caramel used as rules, block fields, stamps, and a committed full-section field. White photo mats, hairline ledger rules, tabular serif numerals, large Cormorant Garamond display with italic emphasis, DM Sans for quiet UI text. Components stay rectangular with almost no radius; shadows are soft and offset.
